or confirmations. Every item has an owner — a blank owner is escalated, not left. Dates
are absolute (Buzz event timestamps or the date raised in-channel). "Resolved" means
the decision/confirmation has been made — where mechanical execution is still
outstanding, the resolution text says so explicitly rather than implying "done."
